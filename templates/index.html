Diagnosis

Growth masks a broken fulfilment loop: repeat purchase fell from 41% to 27%, while cancellations rose to 11%. Product unavailability drives 35% of cancellations, and stale manual inventory is the root cause. Local discovery is the defensible advantage—if customers can trust it.

Product logic

LocalTrust combines freshness, stock, store reliability and workload into an availability-confidence score. Shoppers see honest choices before checkout; stores update only high-demand products and confirm orders from one queue.

Six-month target

Reduce unavailable-product cancellations by 30%, cut related support tickets by 20%, and lift second-order conversion by 5 percentage points. This shifts promotional spend toward retention without warehouses or deep discounts.
